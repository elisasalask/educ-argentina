# First comparison of teaching career rules

The pilot supports a more nuanced starting point than “the statutes reward only seniority.” The supplied primary texts contain competitive recruitment, professional appraisal and promotion mechanisms involving more than years of service. This is a description of formal provisions in the supplied corpus; it does not show their implementation, their effectiveness or their current legal force.

## What the pilot covers

Buenos Aires, Misiones and Córdoba primary education form the comparable three-province core. A separate Córdoba secondary case tests branch differences. There are 16 indicators across the assignment's four dimensions, with 64 case-level decisions. Article and PDF-page evidence is available for each row in the review table. Sources were coded on 9 November 2026; the documents themselves reflect different periods. In particular, Córdoba's 2024 payment conditions must not be projected backward onto 2023 survey data.

## What changed in the codebook during the pilot

1. **The unit is province × branch × indicator.** Córdoba's primary and secondary rules must not be collapsed. Their printed nationality conditions, entry methods, promotion thresholds and study-leave wording differ.
2. **Document names do not establish instrument type.** The professionality file is an official bulletin containing Economy Ministry Resolution 7/2024, not a standalone decree. The leave booklet is a UEPC guide, not the leave law itself.
3. **Repeal is not absence.** Córdoba's repealed pay provisions mean that outside instruments are needed, not that the province lacks seniority increments. The same issue arises in reconstructing primary promotion criteria.
4. **Recognition needs several indicators.** Promotion tests, annual appraisal, professional development credit, seniority pay and training/attendance pay operate through different mechanisms.
5. **A classroom pathway needs a narrow definition.** More hours, a better location and a coordinator position may be important but do not by themselves establish graded advanced classroom-teacher ranks.
