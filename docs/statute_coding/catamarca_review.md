Added on 9 November 2026 as dataset revision **0.2.1**, using the existing **0.2.0** codebook. Catamarca adds 16 primary/general-provision decisions and 52 Spanish excerpts from one 144-page compilation. The earlier 80 decisions are unchanged. No new descriptive categories were necessary.

Open [the unified review filtered to Catamarca](statute_review.html#case=CAT_PRI). Expand each evidence panel to inspect the quoted Spanish and open the local PDF at its one-based page number. These are findings from the supplied documents; current legal force and implementation remain unverified.

## Review these distinctions first

1. **PR01 — leadership competitions and terms.** The PDF includes Law 5103, not just the older statute. Its articles 1–4 prescribe antecedents, an opposition examination and an interview based on an institutional leadership/supervision project. A four-year appointment can be renewed once for another four years if the project-objective evaluation is passed; a further extension requires a new competition. The supplied text calls this titular status for that term. Do not describe every leadership appointment as indefinite. Evidence: PDF pp. 141–142.
2. **PR02 and MP02 — older thresholds remain unresolved.** Older articles 83–89 print role-specific service requirements, and articles 28(c)/91(b) print a Muy Bueno appraisal threshold. Law 5103 article 10 suspends conflicting promotion provisions; article 2(b) places experience, antecedents and credential requirements in the competition bases. Those bases and the implementing regulation are missing. Both indicators are therefore **unclear**, with the older rules retained as historical context. The separate evaluation for leadership-term renewal is explicit and remains recorded in MP02.
3. **AS02 and AS03 — a limited alternative titularization route.** Appended Decree-Law 3267 article 2 allows interim teachers in inhospitable-zone schools to obtain titular status without a competition after an uninterrupted school year, conditional on efficiency, adaptation to the environment, social integration and an on-site assessment. This is not an automatic route for all interim teachers. Evidence: PDF p. 139.
4. **AS01 and AS03 — historical restrictions need verification.** The supplied text prints nationality, departmental domicile and primary-entry age conditions. The compilation also retains the original transitory article 173, suspending stability while Law 3090 remains in effect; the later 2000 disciplinary regulation expressly recognizes titular stability. Record this history without assuming the old suspension or the printed restrictions apply today. Evidence: PDF pp. 6–8, 19, 41 and 80.
5. **CT04 — continuity is different from a single appointment’s duration.** Resolution 723/2001 details ranked hub-school assemblies and a same-school/shift/year-section continuity exception. Its 90-day annual service sequence does not mean a replacement continues after the replaced teacher returns. Evidence: PDF pp. 72–77.
