# CABA — next jurisdiction for team review

CABA contributes 16 primary/general-provision decisions to the unified database. Fifteen have explicit supplied provisions; one is unclear because the supplied versions describe different appraisal-related cessation procedures. These statuses describe documentary evidence, not policy quality.

## What to review first

1. **MP04: classroom career stage.** Articles 9 and 25 explicitly list Maestro/a Especialista in the primary ladder, separately from Maestro/a Coordinador/a de Trayectorias Escolares. This justifies adding `classroom_specialist_stage` to the codebook. It does not demonstrate actual appointments or impact.
2. **AS04: version conflict.** The 2022 regulation describes improvement training before possible disciplinary proceedings after deficient appraisal. The supplied annex labeled 2026 describes an administrative cessation route with notification and response. Its cover lacks the adopting-act number/date. Obtain that instrument before resolving applicable law.
3. **PR01: promotion courses.** The 2022 regulation scores the course numerically; the annex labeled 2026 describes pass/fail access to the examination. The master records both versions without merging their scoring rules.
4. **PR04: training pay.** Article 78 authorizes ministry salary incentives. This is not evidence of an implemented payment, an amount, or performance-based pay.
5. **CT02/CT04: detailed training points and temporary allocation.** Detailed 2026 provisions are recorded with an adoption caveat. They should not be presented as independently verified current rules.

## How to review

Open [the unified review page](statute_review.html), select **CABA · Primary**, and expand the Spanish evidence under each decision. PDF links open the cited page of the local supplied file. Check the summary, scope, exceptions and evidence together. Record proposed corrections before adding another jurisdiction.

The database contains supplied-text coding only. It does not establish current legal force, implementation, or inter-coder agreement. The other 20 jurisdictions remain uncoded.
