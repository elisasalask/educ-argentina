Entry eligibility

Entry as titular requires a qualifying teaching credential, fitness and conduct conditions, and statutory eligibility requirements. The text allows foreign applicants subject to residence and Spanish requirements.

Exceptions and limits: Article 57 also prints an initial-entry age ceiling of 50 with exceptions. Record it as supplied text, not a verified restriction in force in 2026; a transitory credential exception is also printed.

Codebook at a glance

Codes describe mechanisms, not policy quality. See the CSV codebook and protocol for permitted values and status definitions.

CodeIndicatorDecision rule
AS01Entry eligibilityRecord credentials, nationality/residence and other restrictions; preserve exceptions. Do not infer contemporary enforceability.
AS02Recruitment procedureDistinguish a credentials/antecedents competition from an obligatory examination. Record priority rules and exceptional routes.
AS03Stability and confirmationRecord titular versus temporary scope; do not equate a general stability clause with immediate tenure for every employee.
AS04Loss of stability and safeguardsRecord performance, discipline and redundancy separately. Due process is not proof of implementation or an absolute dismissal prohibition.
PR01Promotion mechanismFocus on hierarchy, not ordinary transfers. Record separate governing laws and exceptions.
PR02Promotion experience thresholdsRecord thresholds as printed by target role and branch. Missing implementing law means incomplete coverage, not no threshold.
PR03Seniority remunerationRecord percentage, service years and calculation base. Do not confuse statutory schedule percentages with current take-home pay.
PR04Training or attendance pay incentiveCode monetary conditions separately from teaching-performance evaluation, absence deductions, geography pay and promotion points.
MP01Periodic professional appraisalRecord appraiser, evidence and scope; an annual requirement does not demonstrate actual appraisal quality.
MP02Appraisal and advancementDistinguish eligibility threshold, ranking factor, and removal sanction. Do not infer that removal provisions determine promotion.
MP03Appraisal review and appealRecord authority, route and statutory deadline where available. Do not transfer deadlines across procedures.
MP04Advanced classroom career stagesRequires a formal higher classroom-teacher grade while retaining ordinary teaching as the core role. Seniority pay, more hours, transfers, coordinators and management posts do not alone qualify.
CT01Professional development supportRecord teacher obligations and authority support separately; a policy aspiration is not a guaranteed annual training allocation.
CT02Recognition of trainingSeparate the right to study from points/credit for completed training. Record where scoring is delegated.
CT03Leave and study leaveDistinguish a statutory right, discretionary permission, and a union guidance summary. Do not infer days from an absent regulation.
CT04Substitute coverageRecord eligibility, selection, coverage timing and cessation. A short absence threshold is not the same as a prohibition on replacement.
